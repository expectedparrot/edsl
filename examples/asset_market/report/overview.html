How to read the comparison

  • Baseline: no behavioral persona beyond the common profit objective.
  • BT+OC+PC: four bubble timers, four overconfident contrarians, and four precommitters. Text reconstructed from the slide summaries.
  • Evolved: seven Trend Readers, three Noise, and two Reversion Riders, using the supplied verbatim persona texts.
  • The source reports mean peak fractions of 0.628 for same-market humans, 0.627 for the highlighted theory mix, and 0.633 for evolved personas. These are external summary benchmarks. Their raw price paths are unavailable, and none are plotted as reconstructed human observations.

Execution and evidence

Every period fans out to 12 private trader tasks, waits for all sealed orders, clears one uniform price, and atomically updates balances and income in shared state. The exchange uses deterministic code; only traders use the language model.

SQLite workflow and state histories, raw model calls, serialized definitions, accepted Results packages, and order/period CSVs are saved under examples/asset_market/runs/gpt-4o-mini-pilot. See the example README for every reconstruction choice and reproducible commands.
