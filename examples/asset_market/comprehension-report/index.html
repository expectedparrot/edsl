Explicit comprehension questions ask for objective arithmetic and rules. Passing them does not demonstrate that the same model spontaneously applies that knowledge while trading. A low bid can be a valid strategy, so we do not score low bids as comprehension failures. An order that sells more shares than the trader owns violates the submitted-order constraint, even though the exchange later caps it.
