01 / Findings

No bubble emerged. The main outcome was a failure to form a two-sided market.

The baseline and evolved-persona markets completed all 30 periods without a transaction. The BT+OC+PC market traded one share at $14.25 in period 2 and three shares at $14.00 in period 3. Its remaining 28 periods had no trades. These prices stayed close to the $14 fundamental value.

This pilot therefore did not reproduce the source presentation’s bubble-and-crash result. It did reproduce the experimental sequence: traders observed their private accounts and the public tape, submitted sealed orders and forecasts, and received the resulting fills, interest, dividends, and terminal redemption. The absence of trades is a substantive outcome of these submitted decisions, not evidence that the exchange stopped running.

02 / Design and execution

One market, one shared ledger, 30 settlement barriers.

Economic environment

  • Each trader starts with $100 cash and four shares.
  • Post-trade cash earns 5% interest each period.
  • Each post-trade share pays a common dividend of $0.40 or $1.00, equally likely.
  • After period 30’s income, shares are redeemed for $14 and all cash is retained.
  • One buy, sell, or hold decision per trader per period; integer quantities; no borrowing or short selling.

Conditions and information

  • Baseline: a common profit objective without a behavioral persona.
  • Theory: four bubble timers, four overconfident contrarians, four precommitters.
  • Evolved: seven Trend Readers, three Noise traders, two Reversion Riders.
  • Each trader sees its persona, own complete decision/fill history, own cash and shares, past transaction history, and remaining horizon.
Private observation→12 sealed submissions→Uniform-price clearing→Interest + dividends→Next period

The workflow waits for all 12 committed submissions before the exchange settles. Shared-state transactions admit orders and update accounts atomically. The next period cannot open until settlement finishes. The exchange is deterministic code; only the traders call the language model. Current-period orders and other traders’ accounts remain hidden from traders.

Matching sorts bids from highest to lowest and asks from lowest to highest. The largest set of crossing unit orders trades at the midpoint of the last matched bid and ask, rounded half up to cents. Seeded priority breaks equal-price ties. Unmatched orders expire. Buy quantities are capped by cash at the submitted limit price and the market’s 48-share supply; sells are capped by inventory. The audit found no rejected orders and two size reductions in the theory session.

The seed is 140926 in each condition. It controls the dividend sequence, seat assignment, and price-tie priority; it does not make hosted model generation deterministic. The same dividend sequence permits a transparent accounting comparison across these three sessions.

06 / Portfolios and payoffs

Wealth rose even where no one traded.

Every nontrading trader finished with $648.69, up from initial wealth of $156 when the four endowed shares are valued at $14 each. This growth came from compounded cash interest, the realized dividends, and $56 redemption of the initial holdings. It cannot be attributed to trading skill.

@@PORTFOLIOS@@

The four traders who participated in a trade finished between $636.77 and $658.82. The buyer of three shares finished $11.92 below the same-dividend no-trade path; the two sellers finished $7.93 and $10.13 above it. These realized differences reflect transaction cash flows, subsequent interest, and the dividend path. They are not expected-return estimates or causal persona effects.

07 / Forecasts and stated reasoning

Different forecasts were not enough to create exchange.

The baseline forecasts were pinned to the $14 benchmark. Theory and evolved personas produced dispersion in forecasts and quotes, but the evolved population never supplied a seller. Quotes are intentions to trade at a limit price; neither a high forecast nor a high bid is a transaction.

@@BELIEFS@@

Forecast accuracy can be evaluated only when the target period has an observed transaction. Baseline and evolved sessions have no such target prices. The theory session supplies only the two observed clearing prices, so forecast errors rest on very limited support. Forecasts beyond the terminal period were instructed to use $14 and are excluded from horizon-accuracy comparisons. See the accuracy export for exact sample counts.

09 / Interpretation and limits

A successful execution is not a successful behavioral replication.

The presentation reports mean peak timing near 0.63 of the horizon for its highlighted theory and evolved populations. In this pilot, two populations have no price peak because they have no transactions. The theory market’s earliest maximum occurs in period 2 (2/30 ≈ 0.067), but a $14.25 price followed by $14.00 is not evidence of the large bubble studied in the source. No transaction reaches the appendix’s 1.25 × fundamental threshold ($17.50).

  • Model substitution: this run uses gpt-4o-mini, not the model shown in the source’s experimental figures.
  • Prompt reconstruction: the evolved persona texts are supplied verbatim; theory texts were reconstructed from short slide descriptions. The common profit objective and accounting presentation are implementation choices.
  • Mechanism choices: initial $14 reference, one order per trader, midpoint clearing, holding as an action, tie priorities, income ordering, and cent rounding were explicitly chosen where the slides were incomplete.
  • Information and memory: agents see complete own histories in repeated single-response prompts. The source does not specify an identical memory interface.
  • Sample size: one session per treatment cannot estimate between-market uncertainty. Traders and periods within a market are interdependent observations.
  • Unperformed work: no original human microdata, no re-estimation of behavioral coefficients, no evolutionary search, and no executed 315-market composition sweep.

Useful follow-up experiments would vary model, initialization, and wording separately, measure comprehension and persona compliance, and repeat each market across independent sessions. Those are proposed follow-ups; none are silently folded into the results shown here.

10 / Evidence and reproducibility

Audited from the submitted decision to terminal cash.

The report reconstructs all 1,080 opening and closing portfolios from saved fills and income. It checks each reconstructed terminal balance against the execution ledger. Seeded counterparty matching reproduces every observed fill. The execution audit verifies exactly 360 submissions and 30 settlements per market; all 390 workflow attempts per market succeeded.

Private-state audits compare every observed account with the correct trader’s reconstructed opening balance and history. Public views contain only previously completed periods. Raw system prompts include each assigned persona. Saved Results packages contain 360 responses per market. The implementation test suite also covers budget constraints, sealed visibility, order-arrival invariance, and recovery after a state commit whose workflow acknowledgement was interrupted.
