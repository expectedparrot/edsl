Expected Parrot provides tools for designing, running, and analyzing research with humans and AI. Conduct surveys, interviews, experiments, simulations, and other research using the Research Agent, visual Builder, or open-source Python package, EDSL.

All three approaches use EDSL, so studies can be inspected, shared, reproduced, and extended in code.

## Ways to work with Expected Parrot

<CardGroup cols={3}>
<Card title="Research Agent" icon="comments" href="/en/latest/research_agent">

With the Research Agent, you can review and refine a study as it develops, inspect the underlying EDSL code, and share the session with collaborators. Use the Builder for direct editing, or work independently in Python notebooks with EDSL. Expected Parrot also provides hosted execution, caching, storage, and collaboration tools.

## Getting started

[Create an Expected Parrot account](https://www.expectedparrot.com/login) and choose how you would like to work:

- **Start with the Research Agent:** [Open a session](https://www.expectedparrot.com/research-agent), describe what you want to research, or use a [playbook](/en/latest/research_agent#using-playbooks) for a particular project or method.
- **Build a study manually:** [Open the Builder](https://www.expectedparrot.com/polly) to create or import a survey or interview. See the [Builder guide](/en/latest/survey_builder).
- **Work in Python:** Install EDSL with `pip install edsl` and follow [EDSL Getting Started](/en/latest/getting_started) and the [Starter Tutorial](/en/latest/starter_tutorial).

You can move between these approaches as your research develops.

## Research with humans and AI

Collect responses from human participants through surveys and AI-led interviews, run simulations with AI respondents, or design studies that compare both. Human research can be the starting point and purpose of a study.

Use simulations to explore hypotheses, pretest instruments, and examine model behavior. AI-generated responses do not automatically represent real populations; compare them with human evidence when evaluating what they can tell you.

## Academic research and teaching

We support academic researchers, instructors, and students using Expected Parrot for research and coursework. Academic users can access the platform and receive an initial allocation of usage credits. Sign up with your academic email and [contact us](mailto:info@expectedparrot.com) for support. Playbooks provide reusable instructions for research methods, projects, and course assignments.

See [EDSL in Research](/en/latest/papers), the [Teaching guide](/en/latest/teaching), and [how to cite EDSL](/en/latest/citation).
